# Changelog

Newest first.

## 2026-11-22

- License changed from MIT to GPL v3 or later.
- sdict: *por que* (no accent) now shows *por qué* ("why") first, then *porque* and *porqué*.
- sdict: set phrases (*por qué*, *de nada*, *muy bien*) show their own entry instead of a word-by-word breakdown.
- sdict: short forms get their own meaning in breakdowns (*muy* → "very").
- Shorter README.

## 2026-11-20

- SDict.app translates whole sentences on-device, Spanish ↔ English (macOS 26+).
- sdict: word-by-word sentence breakdowns with dictionary forms and grammar.
- sdict: common words rank first (*tiendas* → *tienda*, not *tender*).
- sdict: English loanwords show the Spanish translation first (*hot* → *caliente*).
- New hotkey ⌃⌥⇧S opens an empty SDict search window.
- New tool: **ssay**, Spanish speech with Google voices or the macOS voice.
- stop-billing: optional Cloud Function that cuts off Google billing past a budget.
- First release: jdict, jsay, jsent and sdict, with the JDict and SDict apps.
